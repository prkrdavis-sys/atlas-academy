Source (via Poly Pizza / Google Poly archive):
- https://poly.pizza/m/5ckRCisrnXh  (Cumulus Clouds 2)
- https://poly.pizza/m/25RmW99gwuv (Cumulus Clouds 5)
- https://poly.pizza/m/3wzu2FRYXUi (Cumulus Clouds 3)

Files in this folder are normalized unit-box meshes derived from the
original OBJ geometry (centered, scaled to max dimension = 1, normals
recomputed). Atlas Academy applies its own white translucent materials
and places instances into WMO altitude bands on the globe.

Attribution required when redistributing.
