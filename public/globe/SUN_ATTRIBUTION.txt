sun-glow.png — soft radial falloff used for corona wash layers.

Source imagery: NASA Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO) / AIA 171 Å
Latest feed example: https://sdo.gsfc.nasa.gov/assets/img/latest/latest_1024_0171.jpg
License: Public domain (U.S. government work / NASA imagery)
NASA media use: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The disk is color-graded and composited with an original soft circular corona
for the Atlas Academy globe; NASA owns the source plate. Alpha is forced to
zero before the frame edge so no square outline appears in additive blending.
