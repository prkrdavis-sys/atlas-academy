hurricane-florence-density.png — transparent storm-density texture derived from a NOAA GOES-East satellite image of Hurricane Florence.

Source image: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Florence_Strengthens_to_a_Category_4_Hurricane_(29678024287).png
Direct source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Florence%202018-09-10%201400Z.png
Source credit: NOAA / CIRA
License: Public domain

The source was cropped around the storm, resized to 512x512, and converted to a
grayscale RGBA density mask. Dark ocean pixels become transparent; bright cloud
tops become denser. The processed texture is used as a shape mask only, with
color and atmospheric lighting supplied by the globe shader.
